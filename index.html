03

Strategy modifiers

Extra effort for the kind of project this is; the four modifiers stack. Client and coordination: 1 adds nothing, 5 adds h a week to every phase in scope at that phase's rate. Approvals: each level above 1 adds a review period after SD, DD and CD (schedule risk). Redesign: each level above 1 adds % of SD, DD and CD effort as a rework allowance.

Review periods and the strategy allowance apply automatically to both the expanded fee and the top-down estimate — there's nothing to toggle.
